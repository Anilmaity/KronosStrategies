## The finding that reorders everything: the harness look-ahead

Two agents (`r2_c03`, `r2_s14`) independently found that `lab/harness.py` handed every
strategy the M5/M15 bar **in progress** on 4 of 5 M1 ticks — its close/high/low up to 4 min
(M15: 14 min) in the future — while the live runner only ever sees `complete` candles.
Proof: closed-bar alignment reproduces **6/6** of c03's live signals and **86 %** of s14's
May–June signal rows; the leaky slice 2/6 and 61 %. Confirmed by the coordinator on the
Stage-1 lists (c03: 69 % of entries at the leaky minute; s14 PF 1.87 there vs 0.91 at the
honest one). Fixed in `1c9ae30` (`tests/test_harness_closed_bars.py`: 80 % / 93 % leaky → 0 %).

## What round 2 established beyond round 1

The ICT concepts, tested mechanically with controls under honest information, do not carry
an edge on gold at these stop distances — and the two strategies the project believed did
were reading the future. What *is* real, measured: the live gates protect; live fills are
better than nominal; the copy-trader is the drag; the manager's sim was right. The next
edge, if there is one, has to be found on the fixed harness with the parity gate in place.
